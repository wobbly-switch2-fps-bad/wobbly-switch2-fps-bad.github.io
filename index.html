How this was measured
  • Each capture was decoded frame by frame. A frame counts as new if more than 0.2% of its pixels changed by more than 6 brightness levels from the frame before it. Repeated frames show 0.00% change, so they separate cleanly from new ones.
  • Average fps = unique frames ÷ clip length. "Worst 1s" is the fewest unique frames in any 1-second window.
  • The results stay within about 1 fps when the threshold is changed.
  • The recorder tops out at 30 fps and sometimes skipped capture slots (up to ~20% in clip 9), so treat these numbers as a floor. The game may have run slightly faster in clips 3, 4, 5 and 9.
  • The videos are the original recordings, repackaged from .mov to .mp4 without re-encoding.
